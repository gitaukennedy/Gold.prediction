✨ WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1️⃣  FETCHES DATA
    • Connects to Yahoo Finance
    • Downloads 3 months of gold futures (GC=F) data
    • Extracts OHLCV (Open, High, Low, Close, Volume)

2️⃣  PROCESSES DATA
    • Calculates 15+ technical indicators:
      ✓ RSI (Relative Strength Index)
      ✓ MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
      ✓ Moving Averages (20, 50-day)
      ✓ Daily Returns & Volatility
      ✓ Lagged Features (for time-series prediction)
    • Handles missing values
    • Scales features for ML

3️⃣  TRAINS MODEL
    • Splits data: 80% train, 20% test
    • Trains Random Forest Classifier
    • Predicts next-day price direction (UP or DOWN)
    • Reports: Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score

4️⃣  GENERATES SIGNALS
    • Analyzes latest market data
    • Produces trading signals:
      📈 BUY  - Predicted UP + RSI < 70 + High confidence
      📉 SELL - Predicted DOWN + RSI > 30 + High confidence
      ⏸️ HOLD - Low confidence or conflicting signals

5️⃣  EXECUTES TRADES
    • Buys when BUY signal generated
    • Sells when SELL signal generated
    • Risk Management:
      🛑 Stop Loss: 2% below entry
      🎯 Take Profit: 3% above entry
    • Tracks all trades and P&L

6️⃣  REPORTS RESULTS
    • Total trades executed
    • Win rate (% profitable trades)
    • Total profit/loss
    • Average profit per trade
    • Portfolio return %
